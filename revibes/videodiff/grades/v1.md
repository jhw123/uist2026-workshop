# Graded rubric: VideoDiff
Score 0.97: 14 full, 1 partial, 0 failure, 0 not graded, of 15 tests.

## A. Generating alternatives

### V-01 · Uploading a video gives ten rough cuts in a versions outline
Steps: Upload or pick the pheasant video. Look at the left panel and the list of versions.
Expected (full success): Ten rough-cut versions appear, each with its own video player. A versions outline lists them all by ID, duration and number of sections (e.g., "#5 | 2:04 | 6 Sections").
Grade: full

### V-02 · The rough cuts really differ in length and coverage
Steps: Compare the durations and section counts of the ten versions in the outline.
Expected (full success): Durations spread clearly (some under 2 minutes, some 2 to 4, some close to the full video), and section counts differ (some focus on one or two sections, others take short parts from many).
Grade: full

### V-03 · Each version plays its own edited video
Steps: Play a short version from the start and watch past the first cut.
Expected (full success): The player shows the version's own duration and plays only the selected parts, jumping over what was cut.
Grade: full

## B. Timeline view

### V-04 · Sections come from the source and are shared by every version
Steps: In the timeline view, look at the section bars of three or four versions.
Expected (full success): Each version shows a colored, labelled section bar with a filmstrip of video thumbnails. The same section has the same name and color in every version, and bar widths match the time each section takes.
Grade: full

### V-05 · Hover a section to see thumbnails. Click to play from there
Steps: Hover over a section in one version's timeline, then click a point inside it.
Expected (full success): Hovering shows thumbnails of that part. Clicking plays that version's video from the clicked point.
Grade: full

### V-06 · Edited vs. source timeline aligns versions to the source
Steps: Switch from the edited (extracted) timeline to the source (original) timeline. Hover a lighter part.
Expected (full success): All versions redraw on the same source time axis, so included parts line up across versions. Excluded parts appear as lighter boxes, and hovering one shows its thumbnails and topic keywords.
Grade: full

## C. Transcript view

### V-07 · Transcript view by section, with bold visual keywords
Steps: Switch to the transcript view. Read one version, then click a section heading.
Expected (full success): Each version's transcript is split under section headings colored as in the timeline, with a mini timeline. Visually concrete words (e.g., "breast", "celery", "stock pot") are in bold. Clicking a heading plays from that section's start.
Grade: full

### V-08 · Source transcript, side by side, with synchronized scrolling
Steps: In the transcript view, switch to source. Scroll one version's transcript.
Expected (full success): Each version shows the full source transcript with its excluded text visibly set apart (e.g., unhighlighted). Versions sit side by side and scroll together.
Grade: full

## D. Organizing

### V-09 · Sort versions by stage-specific criteria
Steps: Sort rough cuts by duration, then by number of sections. In a later stage (V-14), open the sort menu again.
Expected (full success): The list and outline reorder correctly. Rough cuts sort by duration and number of sections, and B-roll or text-effect versions sort by number of effects and media type or style.
Grade: full

### V-10 · Pin to the top, archive to the bottom
Steps: Pin one version from the middle of the list. Archive another.
Expected (full success): The pinned version moves to the top and the archived one to the bottom, in both the main list and the outline, and they stay there after sorting.
Grade: full

## E. Customizing with prompts

### V-11 · Edit a version with a prompt. The result sits below it with a summary
Steps: On one rough cut, click Edit and type "Shorten the part about making stock." Compare the result with the original.
Expected (full success): A new version (e.g., "#3-Edited") appears directly below the original with a short description of what changed. Its timeline shows the stock section actually shorter and the rest unchanged.
Grade: partial

### V-12 · Generate a new version from a prompt, or "Surprise me"
Steps: Click Generate New and ask for "A version under one minute about searing the breast." Then try Surprise me.
Expected (full success): Each makes a new version that follows the prompt (or differs from the existing ones for Surprise me), pinned at the top.
Grade: full

### V-13 · Recombine versions by their IDs
Steps: Write a prompt such as "Use the opening of #2 and the ending of #7."
Expected (full success): A new version appears whose timeline visibly takes the named parts from the named versions.
Grade: full

## F. B-roll and text effects stages

### V-14 · B-roll stage: ten versions built on the chosen rough cut
Steps: Choose a rough cut and generate B-roll. Hover a B-roll thumbnail, click it, then open the transcript view.
Expected (full success): Ten versions appear, all on the same rough-cut timeline, differing in the number and kind of B-roll, with real images or clips from the B-roll library. Each B-roll shows above the timeline bar as a thumbnail with its search keyword. Hovering shows the scene it covers. Clicking plays the video with the B-roll laid over it. The transcript highlights the sentences that have B-roll.
Grade: full

### V-15 · Text effects stage: keywords, not thumbnails
Steps: Choose a B-roll version and generate text effects. Hover a keyword on the timeline, then click it.
Expected (full success): Ten versions appear on top of the chosen B-roll version. Each shows its text effects as keywords on the timeline. Hovering shows the narration sentence. Clicking plays the video with the styled text on screen. Versions differ in style (title, subtitle, lower third) and number.
Grade: full
