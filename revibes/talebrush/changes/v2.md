Built by Claude Code (Claude Opus 5.5) from the paper.
Version 1 was graded full on all 15 tests (graded by the agent, not by people).
Version 2 therefore fixes no failed tests. It addresses three problems noted in
"What we saw": a stray click could regenerate a sentence, the widest band
needed a very fast swipe, and the surprise effect was modest.

## Changed
- **T-11:** a click or very short stroke on the canvas no longer counts as a
  redraw. Before, touching a slot's centre regenerated that sentence. Now a
  stroke must be at least 12 columns long (about 2% of the canvas width) and
  cover at least 40% of a slot to regenerate it.
- **T-05:** sketching speed reaches the widest band with an ordinary quick
  swipe. The speed at which the band is widest dropped from 1.4 to 0.9 canvas
  units/ms. Slow drawing is unchanged.
- **T-13:** stronger surprise instructions to the model. Low-surprise slots must
  be plain and predictable, high-surprise slots must contain an event the
  reader would never see coming, and surprise is independent of fortune.

## Instructions from the group
- Same as v1: the agent runs all six steps and grades on the group's behalf.
  The agent had read the rubric before building.

## Assumptions
- Unchanged from v1.

## Substitutions
- Unchanged from v1. The LLM bridge (Claude Haiku) stands in for GPT-Neo soft
  prompts and GeDi, and surprise is a prompt instruction, not temperature.

## Still missing
- Speed: one generation takes about 30–60 s through the bridge (one generation
  call, one recognition call, plus up to two retry rounds). The paper's local
  models were faster.
- The crowdsourced fortune dataset, soft-prompt training and the technical
  evaluation (Sections 5.1, 6) are out of scope.
